We already have thousands players registered for the Playtest, and we expect a lot of new players to join in for the event! This is a great opportunity to showcase your team's (or solo's) work and game-modes to a large audience, and get feedback from the community!

## Network Packet Bundling {/* #network-packet-bundling */}

We introduced **experimental** network packet bundling: network packets are now merged together before flushing, reducing the amount of packets being sent over the network.

In our tests, this has reduced the number of packets sent by up to **~95%** in a server with hundreds of players, which dramatically helps the server performance and mitigate some connection issues.

## Scripting Improvements {/* #scripting-improvements */}

### Water Enter/Leave Events {/* #water-enterleave-events */}

We added new <EventReference type="Class" class_name="Actor" event="EnterWater" /> and <EventReference type="Class" class_name="Actor" event="LeaveWater" /> events, triggered whenever an Actor enters or leaves a body of water. Only Actors that simulate physics trigger these, such as <Classes.Character />, <Classes.Prop />, all <Classes.Vehicle /> and all <Classes.Pickable />.

```lua title="Server/Index.lua" showLineNumbers
Prop.Subscribe("EnterWater", function(prop)
	Console.Log(prop:GetID() .. " entered the water!")
end)
```


### Buoyancy Customization {/* #buoyancy-customization */}

<Classes.Prop />, <Classes.Pickable /> and <Classes.Vehicle /> got two new methods to tweak how they float:

- <MethodReference type="Class" class_name="Prop" method_name="SetBuoyancySettings" show_class_name />
- <MethodReference type="Class" class_name="Prop" method_name="SetBuoyancyPontoons" show_class_name />

`SetBuoyancySettings` lets you enable/disable buoyancy forces entirely, or tweak the buoyancy coefficient, while `SetBuoyancyPontoons` lets you override the spherical pontoons used for the Water simulation, instead of the ones automatically calculated from the mesh bounds and mass.

:::note

**Pontoons** and **Buoyancy** Settings only have an effect on Maps with [`enable_water_buoyancy`](/docs/next/core-concepts/packages/packages-guide#settings-detailed) enabled.

:::
